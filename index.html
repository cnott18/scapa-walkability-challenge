SCAPA Walkability Challenge

Explore downtown Columbia and help evaluate the pedestrian experience.

How It Works

1. Walk the areaUse the map and your phone's location to follow along as you explore downtown Columbia.
2. Assess a sidewalkChoose a green sidewalk segment, rate its condition, note an issue if present, and optionally take a photo.
3. Add an observationMark a location for a crosswalk, curb ramp, signal, lighting, safety concern, amenity, or other walkability feature.
4. Keep exploringSubmit as many observations and sidewalk assessments as you notice during the challenge.
